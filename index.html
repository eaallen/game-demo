Cypress Bayou Trail

A deep South boardwalk maze over the swamp. The corn snake hides in a clearing in a far corner, and only one winding route reaches it. Right before the viewing deck you cross a bridge over open water, and the snake itself is sprawled sunbathing on a big cypress branch above the deck. Watch for a gator and cottonmouths, find the snake, then follow the orange ribbons back to the car.

How to play

  • You play as Little Eli, starting by the car in the gravel parking area at the south end.
  • ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D to walk.Use the D pad under the screen to walk. Hold two directions at once to walk diagonally.
  • E or SpaceTap the A button (or B) to talk, read signs, look at the corn snake, and get in the car at the end.
  • Tap the Full button at the top right of the game for the big view.
  • Tap Restart at the top right of the game any time to start over from the car.
  • Stay on the boardwalk. The swamp water is blocked off. The maze has junctions, loops, and dead ends, and each dead end has a little sign worth reading. Only one route reaches the hidden clearing.
  • All the animals are real sprite sheets: the alligator swims, the cottonmouths slither, the squirrel runs, and the corn snake lies stretched out sunbathing on a cypress branch over the viewing deck.
  • The last stretch before the deck is a bridge over a wider channel of open swamp water, lighter crosswise planks with rails on both sides. It is the only way onto the deck.
Watch and wait: one big alligator periodically lunges across a boardwalk section on the main route, and three dark cottonmouths slither back and forth across the planks in different corridors. They are easy to see. Wait for a clear gap, then walk through. Touching one costs 1 heart and sends you back to your last safe checkpoint.

Checkpoints: the trailhead on the way out, a rest platform about midway through the maze, and the hidden clearing on the way back. If all 3 hearts run out, you restart by the car with hearts refilled and your corn snake sighting kept.
